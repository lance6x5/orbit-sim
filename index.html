    How it works

    Gravity

    Every body pulls on every other body (N-body gravity). Newton's law says the force between two masses is F = G · m1 · m2 / d2. Dividing by a body's own mass gives its acceleration toward the other one.

    Softening: when two bodies nearly touch, d approaches 0 and the force explodes. We use d2 + ε2 instead of d2, which changes nothing far away but caps the force up close.

    The simulation loop

    Physics runs in fixed time steps (120 per second at 1x), separate from drawing. Each frame, the real time elapsed is added to an accumulator, and as many fixed steps as fit are run. Same input, same result, on any screen refresh rate.

    Integrators

    Euler moves each body along its current velocity, then updates the velocity. Because it always steps along a straight tangent line, orbits gain energy and spiral outward.

    Velocity Verlet includes the curvature term ½ a Δt2 and averages the old and new accelerations. It is time-reversible and symplectic, so energy errors wobble instead of piling up, and orbits stay closed. Watch the Energy drift readout while you switch between them.

    Collisions

    Merge joins bodies, conserving mass and momentum. Bounce applies an elastic impulse. Pass through ignores contact.

    Colors

    Body color follows mass: icy blue (small), teal, gold, orange star, white-hot giant.
